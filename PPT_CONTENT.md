## SLIDE 6 — REVENUE MODEL (Mandatory)

**Title:** Simple, Transparent, Win-Win for Everyone

**Core Model:** 5% Commission on Every Transaction — paid by the Buyer, NOT the Farmer

> *Farmers receive 100% of their listed price. Always.*

### Commission Breakdown

| Revenue Stream | Rate | Description |
|----------------|------|-------------|
| Platform Infrastructure Fee | 3% of deal value | Covers server, development, maintenance |
| Facilitator (Middleman) Fee | 2% of deal value | Logistics, quality verification, dispute resolution |
| **Total Commission** | **5%** | **Absorbed entirely by the Buyer** |

### Example Transaction — 1,000 kg @ ₹50/kg = ₹50,000

- **Farmer receives:** ₹50,000 (full amount — zero deduction)
- **Buyer pays total:** ₹52,500 (₹50,000 + 5%)
- **Platform earns:** ₹1,500 (3%)
- **Middleman earns:** ₹1,000 (2%)

### Why 5% Wins

- Traditional mandis charge **5–8.5%** AND exploit farmers on price
- Competitors charge **8–12%** with no farmer-side protection
- Our model: **Buyer saves 24–45%** | **Farmer earns 20–30% more**
